## Recommendation

**Choose Gossamer if:** you rely on public transit, prefer a polished walkable neighborhood, value unique wellness amenities, and want rent price stability year-over-year.

**Choose Splash if:** you bike or drive, need a larger unit or home office, have a pet, and want to capture the year-one savings — just budget for rent increasing at renewal.
